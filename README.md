## Hi there 👋 I'm Sávia Santos!

I'm a demographer and quantitative researcher interested in population dynamics, environmental change, public health and social inequalities.

My work combines demographic analysis, quantitative methods and spatial data to investigate social and territorial inequalities and inform public policy.

### Research interests
- Demography and population studies
- Indigenous health and sanitation inequalities
- Population, environment and human mobility
- Spatial analysis and evidence-based public policy

### Tools and methods
- **Statistical analysis:** R, Python, Stata
- **Spatial analysis:** QGIS
- **Data visualisation:** Power BI
- **Research practices:** Reproducible workflows, data processing and quantitative analysis

### Featured project

**[Water, Sanitation and Solid Waste for Indigenous Peoples in Brazil](https://github.com/sasaporto/indigenous-wash-census-brazil)**

A reproducible R pipeline using Brazil's 2010 and 2022 Population Censuses to construct water, sewage and solid-waste indicators for Indigenous residents, examine geographical inequalities and assess alternative indicator definitions.
